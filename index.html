Ahmed Alghoraib — Online Arabic, Quran and Tajweed Teacher

I'm Ahmed Alghoraib. I teach Arabic, Quran, Tajweed, and Islamics to English-speaking students — from children learning to read Arabic to adults studying the language or deepening their understanding of Islam. I welcome both Muslims and anyone interested in Islamic culture.

I teach in English and Arabic, and I adjust the balance between the two based on your level. If you're a beginner, we'll use more English to make sure you understand what we're doing; as you progress, I bring in more Arabic so you get used to hearing and using it naturally. The pace is set by you — if something needs more time, we take it; if you want to move faster, we do.

My lessons are one-on-one online, with group classes on Zoom when it suits you better. Sessions run 30 to 60 minutes, and I teach on weekends — Saturday and Sunday.

How a Lesson Works

Arabic Lesson

Core book: العربية بين يديك
  1. Warm-up: a short Arabic conversation, matched to your level, to get into the language.
  2. Lesson: we work through العربية بين يديك, with materials chosen and adapted to what you need.
  3. Wrap-up: a clear summary of what you learned and what to practice before the next lesson.

Quran Session

Memorization & Tajweed together
  1. Review: we go over what you've already memorized.
  2. Correction: I correct mistakes — including ones I may have held back on during the session so as not to interrupt your flow.
  3. New recitation: you recite the new portion you've prepared.
  4. Repetition: we repeat the verses that had mistakes, until they're firm.
  5. New assignment: I introduce the next surah or verses and assign what to memorize for next time.

Tajweed rules are taught alongside the memorization when they're needed — sometimes as a dedicated part of the class, sometimes woven into the recitation itself.
